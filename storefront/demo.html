THE CONDUCTOR’S HANDBOOK

Every ticket is a promise.

Run the last train from Coppergate to Dawn Terminal. Reach the end by 03:00 (six hours after the 21:00 departure). Carry passengers and cargo, keep your promises, and finish with a healthy ledger.

  1. Choose your commitments. Offers show their destination, deadline, space, and fare. Payment arrives at the destination. Late delivery pays half.
  2. Mind your engine. Coal powers every leg. Condition falls with travel. Buy coal or repair before departure. Repairs take 7 minutes.
  3. Choose a track. Express is faster but consumes more coal and condition. Each departure also pays the crew: £10 plus £2 per occupied space. The full cost is shown on each track. The valley siding is economical but slow.
  4. Respond to the night. Incidents between stops offer visible tradeoffs. Resolve them before taking new contracts.
  5. Leave a good record. On-time deliveries earn reputation. Handing a contract to relief loses reputation. A £35 emergency advance is available once; its £45 repayment is deducted in the final result.

Your progress saves automatically in this browser when storage is available. A seed reproduces the same starting journey; your choices change what happens next.

Keyboard: Tab to move between controls, Enter to activate, Escape to close this guide. No account, network connection, or AI service is required to play.
